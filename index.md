# Privacy Policy for City Chime

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Introduction

City Chime ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data
- **How Collected:** We use your device's GPS location (via Geolocator) to provide local news and weather
- **Purpose:** To fetch weather and news relevant to your city
- **Storage:** Location coordinates are cached locally on your device for 15-60 minutes for performance
- **User Control:** You can disable location access at any time in device settings

### Weather Data
- **Source:** Open-Meteo API (free, no API key required)
- **Data Collected:** Latitude and longitude coordinates
- **Retention:** Weather data is cached for 15 minutes and refreshes automatically
- **Privacy:** Open-Meteo does not track individual requests

### Analytics & Crash Reporting
- **Service:** Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics
- **Data:** App usage statistics, error logs, device type, OS version
- **Purpose:** To improve app stability and understand feature usage
- **Retention:** Per Firebase's retention policies

### Device Information
- **Data:** Device model, OS version, app version, device ID
- **Purpose:** For crash reporting and feature compatibility
- **Collection Method:** Automatic via device_info_plus package

### Notifications
- **Service:** Firebase Cloud Messaging
- **Purpose:** To send alarm reminders and app updates
- **Control:** You can disable notifications in app settings

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- Personal information (name, email, phone)
- Health or fitness data
- Browsing history
- Payment information (app is free)
- User content or alarms created

## Data Security

- All location data is processed locally on your device
- No sensitive data is transmitted unencrypted
- We use industry-standard security practices
- Data is never sold to third parties

## Third-Party Services

**Open-Meteo:** Weather API (privacy policy: https://open-meteo.com/en/privacy)
**Firebase:** Analytics and crashlytics (privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
**Geolocator:** Location permission handling (open-source)

## User Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your data
- Request deletion of data
- Disable location, analytics, or notifications anytime
- Contact us about privacy concerns
